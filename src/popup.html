Presto

Step size

How far [ and ] move the speed each press.

Shortcuts

]
Faster by one step
[
Slower by one step
\
Toggle 1x and your last speed
;
Cycle the step size
Works on every video and audio element, in any frame. Speed holds from 0.25x to 16x, and the speed you pick carries over to the next video you open. Press \ to drop back to 1x.
